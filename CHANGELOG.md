# Changelog

What's new in each version of Galley. The website's changelog is built from this file,
and `scripts/publish.sh` turns the Unreleased section into the release notes.

## 0.3.1 · 8 October 2026

Galley now keeps itself up to date.

- Galley checks for new versions once a day and offers to install them, with notes on what's new. Updates are signed, so Galley only installs genuine releases.
- Galley → Check for Updates… looks right away, and Settings → General has options to check automatically or to download and install updates on their own.
- If you're on 0.3.0 or earlier, download this version from the website once; after that, updates arrive in the app.

## 0.3.0 · 8 October 2026

Make the magazine your own.

- A short setup on first launch: name your magazine and, if you like, put your name on the cover.
- Your name prints under the masthead, like a subscriber's copy, in place of the story count. Change it or clear it in Settings → General.
- Longer magazine names are set smaller so they stay on one line.

## 0.2.1 · 1 October 2026

A new icon and a small extraction fix.

- New app icon: two editions on the table.
- Articles show the name readers know ("Wikipedia") instead of a publisher's legal name ("Wikimedia Foundation, Inc.").

## 0.2.0 · 1 October 2026

The first signed and notarised build, ready to download.

- Editions you make yourself (⌘N), by topic or by week, and rename right in the sidebar. Move articles between editions and mark an edition printed.
- Two-column magazine layout, with columns that even out on each article's last page, plus a cover, a contents page and page numbers.
- Share → Galley from Safari, Chrome or any app with a Share menu, and `galley://add` links for Shortcuts.
- Article editor: double-click an article and click any paragraph or picture to leave it out of the printout.
- A cover every time: the lead story's photo, an openly licensed photo from Openverse matched to the edition's name, or one of your own. Covers skip lead images that are mostly text.
- Much faster: an edition lays out in a second or two, and articles arrive in about three.

## 0.1 · 30 September 2026

The first version, published as source code.

- Turns links into articles with WebKit and Mozilla's Readability, and saves each one with its photos on your Mac.
- Lays out editions with a cover, contents and numbered pages, as a PDF for A4 or US Letter.
- The Classic theme, set in Source Serif 4 and Inter, for Latin and Cyrillic text.
- An in-app browser for signing in to sites you subscribe to.
